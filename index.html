What's Karlovac Developers?

Karlovac Developers is a casual, community-run meetup group for developers and tech enthusiasts in Karlovac.

Whether you're a seasoned engineer, a curious beginner, or just here to hang out and for the good vibes — you're welcome here. We meet up, share ideas, hack on stuff, and learn together.

Join Us

We meet regularly to chat, share knowledge, and enjoy each other's company. Drop by our next Meetup to see what it's all about!

You don't need to give a talk — just come hang out, grab a coffee, and meet fellow developers.
