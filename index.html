About us

Building a local tech community, one meetup at a time

Karlovac Developers is a grassroots community of software engineers, designers, and tech enthusiasts based in Karlovac. Since 2017, we've been gathering regularly to share talks, workshops, and ideas — from AI to open source mapping, from .NET to cloud infrastructure.

We believe that great ideas grow best in good company. Whether you're a seasoned engineer or just starting out, you'll find a welcoming crowd here.
