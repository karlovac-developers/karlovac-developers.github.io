## Why host?

Hosting is the simplest way to support the local tech scene, and it puts your team in front of the people building things in Karlovac. It costs you an evening and a room.

## What we need from you

- Room for roughly 15 to 30 people, with somewhere to sit
- A screen or projector
- Wi-Fi for the speakers
- A weekday evening, usually from 18:00
- Ideally somewhere nearby to grab a drink afterwards

## What we bring

- The speakers and the programme for the evening
- Promotion through Meetup and our community chat
- The crowd — our meetups usually draw a dozen or more people
- A room left exactly as we found it

## How it works

- Get in touch and tell us about your space
- We agree on a date that suits you
- We announce the meetup and line up the talks
- You open the door, we handle the rest

## Prefer an unconference?

No speakers needed — the room sets the agenda on the night. Read our [guide to running an unconference](/en/unconference/).
