## The short version

Be kind. Be respectful. Assume good faith. Karlovac Developers is a community of people with a wide range of backgrounds and experience, and we're here to enjoy this together. Harassment and exclusionary behaviour are never acceptable.

## Expected behaviour

- Include others in the conversation and leave room for quieter voices.
- Respect boundaries: ask before taking photos, and accept "no".
- Critique ideas, not people.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Offensive comments about gender, gender identity, age, disability, appearance, nationality or religion.
- Intimidation, stalking, unwelcome physical contact or unwelcome attention.
- Disrupting talks and events.

<Callout tone="warn" title="Reporting">
If you feel unsafe, or see someone else who might be, tell an organizer at the event or email conduct@example.com. Only organizers read reports, and we treat them in confidence.
</Callout>

## Consequences

Organizers may ask for the behaviour to stop immediately, remove a person from the event, or ban them from future events.
