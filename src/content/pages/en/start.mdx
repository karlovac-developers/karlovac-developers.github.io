New faces are the whole point. You do not need to know anyone, and you do not need to be an expert, come as you are.

## What a typical evening looks like

We gather around 18:00, chat over a drink, listen to one or two talks and stay for questions. Everything wraps up by 21:00.

## Join us on Meetup

We post announcements, date changes and venues on [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/karlovac-developers-meetup/).

## Who to look for on arrival

Organizers wear a name badge and are happy to introduce you to people.

## You can just watch

Nobody will put you on the spot. Feel free to sit, listen, and come back next time.
