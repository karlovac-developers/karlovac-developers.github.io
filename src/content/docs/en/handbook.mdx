## Welcome

We hand this handbook to every organizer and volunteer. Read it in order, or jump to the part you need.

<Callout tone="info" title="Our north star">
Everyone who comes should leave feeling welcome and having learned something.
</Callout>

## Roles

- **Evening lead:** opens and closes the meetup, and keeps time.
- **Venue host:** looks after the key, the equipment and the building's rules.
- **Greeter:** welcomes new people at the door.

<Callout tone="tip" title="Two-deep rule">
At least two people can do every role, so a meetup never depends on one person.
</Callout>

## Choosing a venue

We look for a room for 15 to 30 people, with a screen, Wi-Fi and step-free access.

<Callout tone="warn" title="Always visit first">
Before you confirm a venue, see it in person.
</Callout>

## Safety and the Code of Conduct

At the start of every meetup we point to the [Code of Conduct](/en/code-of-conduct/) and who people can talk to.

## After the event

Thank the host and the speakers within 48 hours, and write down what worked and what did not.
