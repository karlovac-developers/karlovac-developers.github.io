Što je nekonferencija?What is an unconference?

Dnevni red ne slažu organizatori, nego sudionici, na početku večeri.The participants, not the organizers, set the agenda, at the start of the evening.

  • Svatko može predložiti temu, a tko je predloži, pokreće razgovor.Anyone can propose a topic; whoever proposes it gets the conversation going.
  • Sesije su uglavnom razgovori, a ne predavanja.Sessions are mostly discussions, not talks.
  • Tko god je došao, pravi su ljudi.Whoever comes are the right people.
  • Zakon dviju nogu: ako ne učite i ne doprinosite, prijeđite tamo gdje možete.The law of two feet: if you’re neither learning nor contributing, move to where you can.
Više: More: citycamp.com/how-to-run-unconference-guide
